Strategy & execution contract

  1. Completed Daily and Weekly structure supplies direction; conflicting bias rejects the setup.
  2. Map resting liquidity on 1-hour and 15-minute candles.
  3. A 5-minute liquidity sweep establishes the raid extreme.
  4. Displacement and a 5-minute close through the opposing swing confirm MSS.
  5. Use an OB/FVG formed inside the sweep-to-MSS leg; validate premium/discount and the retest.
  6. Keep the original structural stop and liquidity targets. The unchanged price buffer is 0.2% of price, with a minimum of 0.01; it was not optimized for forex.

Each source supplies its own warmup and reconstructed 15m/30m/1h/Daily/Weekly candles. No source stitching. Detector bars are available only at candle close; fill is the next available 5-minute open at or after detection. Missing required execution bars produce an unresolved outcome, not an invented fill.

Targets use touches; stops require a completed 5-minute close beyond the level and exit at the next open. Losses can exceed 1R. The shared exit engine checks the target before the stop close on the same bar. The research time stop is ten trading dates. T1/T2/T3 reuse the accepted T2 entry-sanity decision and the same stop. Original target construction can pad sparse liquidity with synthetic RR projections; not every target is a structural liquidity level.

One accepted S16 setup per instrument per New York date. Rejected candidate identities are reconsidered on later scans. There is no cross-strategy arbitration. OHLC cannot reveal the true intrabar quote path. Pip sensitivities are fixed round-trip deductions, not observed broker spread, commission or slippage.
